Using Behavioral Insights to Increase Parents’ Engagement on FLN

Research has shown that parents’ involvement in children’s learning can positively impact their academic performance and social-emotional development. However, parents’ involvement in children’s learning tends to be low because they often lack clarity on the role they can play and feel underconfident in supporting their child, given their own low literacy levels. To solve for this, CSF and CSBC at Ashoka University collaborated to develop behavioral interventions aimed at supporting an increase in parents’ engagement in their child’s Foundational Literacy and Numeracy (FLN) journey.

Playful Progress Towards NIPUN Bharat: Harnessing the Potential of EdTech

This report presents the findings from a recent randomised evaluation conducted in Haryana, India on the use of teacher-directed technology to improve foundational learning at home. Chimple, a Global Learning X-PRIZE finalist, partnered with the Bharti Airtel Foundation’s Satya Bharti Schools program to implement its teacher-led at-home learning model for grades 1 and 2 in five districts of Haryana. An impact evaluation of this programme showed significant and promising improvements in learning, particularly for foundational numeracy. This report synthesises our learnings from the implementation and evaluation of Chimple, and discusses the findings from the impact evaluation to support policy and intervention design.

Report
December 2023
Building Strong Foundations: Examining Early Childhood Education in India

India is home to nearly 8 crore children aged 3-6 years. Research has firmly established the long-term positive effects of investing in high-quality Early Childhood Education (ECE) programmes for children, their families, and society as a whole. While policies for this effort are progressive and far reaching, learning outcomes of children continue to be suboptimal. Our study involved a thorough examination of challenges and opportunities to enhance learning for young children through in-depth curriculum analysis, classroom observations, and interactions with key stakeholders.

Report
April 2026
The Constant and the Variable: Conversations with India’s Mathematical Minds

About half of all Fields Medalists, the highest honour in Mathematics, were once Mathematical Olympiad contestants. Yet fewer than one per cent of eligible students ever participate in the first qualifying examination in India. The challenge is not a lack of talent, but a lack of exposure. This book brings together fifteen conversations with India’s finest mathematical minds, including past IMO medalists and institution-builders, and maps what it takes to bridge the space between talent and opportunity.

Report
April 2026
The Long Bet: How Contemporary Indian Initiatives are Turning Potential into Excellence

This book tells the stories of nine organisations that are working with high-potential students, many from disadvantaged backgrounds, combining intellectual rigour with mentorship, socio- emotional support and real-world opportunity. From schools and enrichment programmes to fellowships that open pathways to higher education, the book reveals the systems that allow talent to flourish: thoughtful identification, committed teachers, near-peer mentors, engaged families and institutions willing to nurture potential over many years.
